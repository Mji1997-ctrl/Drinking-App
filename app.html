How much to trust this

The streets, parks and squares are real OpenStreetMap data, pulled once and built into this page — so the map works with no signal and no roaming. Each venue address was looked up in the same source. Pins marked approx matched only the street, not the house number, so they could be a few hundred metres out. Distances are straight lines between pins, so real walking is a little further. Nothing here confirms a venue is still open or what time it shuts, so tap through to Google Maps or 2GIS and check before you set off.

Rules of the course

How it works

  1. Each pub is a hole. Each hole has a set drink and a par — the number of goes you're allowed to finish it in.
  2. One drink per hole, one hole per pub. Everyone drinks at the same hole.
  3. Count how many goes it took to empty the glass. That's your score, plus penalties.
  4. Lowest total wins. Being over par is not a moral failing, it's just slow.

Penalties

Etiquette

  • Water and food are free. No penalty, no glory, no argument. Eat something at the turn.
  • Nobody gets left behind. The group moves between holes together.
  • Any player may retire. Switch to soft drinks and keep scoring the penalties — the round is the point, not the volume.
  • The scorer's word is final until the next round of drinks, at which point it is negotiable.
